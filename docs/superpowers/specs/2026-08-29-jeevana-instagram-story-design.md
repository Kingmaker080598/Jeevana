# Jeevana Instagram Story Design

## Goal

Create one Instagram Story that introduces Jeevana as a serious civic-design idea, invites viewers to open the working prototype, and acknowledges the Build What Moves India hackathon. The story should make the project worth noticing to Varun Mayya and OpenAI without implying that either party endorses Jeevana.

## Format

- One 9:16 Instagram Story at 1080 × 1920 pixels.
- Preserve comfortable clear space at the top and bottom for Instagram interface controls.
- Use the existing Jeevana visual language: warm paper texture, deep green, marigold accent, black editorial serif headline, thin frame marks, and a dotted journey line.
- Keep the page visually quiet: one hook, one explanatory sentence, one pilot label, one link sticker, and one acknowledgement.

## Approved Composition

1. Top label: `Built for Build What Moves India`
2. Jeevana wordmark and marigold underline.
3. Primary hook in the largest type:

   > What if government services followed life events—not departments?

4. Dotted marigold journey rule.
5. Supporting copy:

   > I built Jeevana to turn scattered portals into one guided path through life’s biggest administrative moments.

6. Pilot label: `Built for India · Piloted in Andhra Pradesh`
7. Instagram Link sticker labeled `Explore Jeevana ↗` and linked to `https://jeevana-brown.vercel.app/`.
8. Closing acknowledgement:

   > Thank you @thevarunmayya for the opportunity — and @openai for helping me bring it to life.

## Tagging and Accuracy

- Use the Instagram handles `@thevarunmayya` and `@openai`.
- Keep `Built for Build What Moves India` visible so “the opportunity” in the acknowledgement has an unambiguous referent.
- Do not use government emblems, official department logos, the OpenAI logo, or language suggesting sponsorship, adoption, selection, or endorsement.
- Jeevana remains an independent hackathon prototype. “Piloted in Andhra Pradesh” describes the prototype’s working journey scope, not a government-operated pilot.

## Interaction

The only action is the Instagram Link sticker. Its visible label is `Explore Jeevana ↗`; its destination is the live Jeevana site. The mentions remain readable and tappable above Instagram’s bottom control area.

## Success Criteria

- A viewer understands the central idea within two seconds.
- Jeevana remains the visual focus; the acknowledgement does not overpower the product message.
- The link sticker is prominent and unobstructed.
- The hackathon, Varun Mayya, and OpenAI are acknowledged accurately.
- The result can be posted as a single Story without requiring a second slide for context.
